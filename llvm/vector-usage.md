# Which vector instructions the compiled kernels use

Generated by `llvm/bench/usage.py` from the blocks in the assembly of 19 programs (the kernels of `docs/encoding-work/arch-data/vec-forms/`: `kernels.c`, `games`, `blas`, `seg`; `llvm/bench/simple.c`; `llvm/tests/vec/*.c`; `llvm/tests/vec_lane_ops.ll`), compiled two ways: **vecloop** = the stage-2 recogniser of SCALAR loops (`clang -O2 -fno-vectorize`, `llc -pdp64-vecloop`): 100 blocks in 95 functions; **lv** = LLVM's own LoopVectorize (`clang -O2 -Xclang -disable-llvm-passes`, `opt -passes='default<O2>' -mtriple=pdp64-unknown-elf`, `llc`; PDP64VecLoop lowers its vector loops): 89 blocks in 87 functions. The words are those of the vector family of the grammar (384 mnemonics). **A static count is a count of instructions in the blocks; the dynamic counts (vector instructions executed, elements) are in `llvm/bench/results.csv` and `llvm/README.md`.** Re-run: `llvm/bench/usage.py --out llvm/vector-usage.md`.

## Instructions no kernel used, and why (what a compiler would need)

* `vlen` (block, 6 words): only needed by a program that must know VL (explicit tail folding, VP intrinsics, approach (c)); the block has hardware strip mining and the lowering never asks
* `vlseg3` (segment, 22 words): a three-field load group (rgb to planes): no kernel of the set has one
* `vldx` (gather, 22 words): a gather (k_hist, k_ssm): LoopVectorize cannot vectorise them (memory conflicts, a call), and the cost model says gathers are not legal (isLegalMaskedGather is false: the lowering does not recognise llvm.masked.gather)
* `vabdu` (lane operations, 4 words): the kernels take `abs(a - b)` of widened bytes: the signed `vabd` serves
* `vmla` (multiply-add, 4 words): integer multiply-add by a scalar: the lowering emits `vmul` then `vadd` for integers; not fused
* `vfmab` (multiply-add, 2 words): the broadcast multiply-add of a GEMM micro-kernel: needs a loop nest, not an innermost loop
* `vdot` (int8 dot, 1 words): int8 dot products (k_q8dot, k_q4dot): LoopVectorize widens the bytes to 32 bits and the lowering matches vacc on widened lanes; the vdot pattern (a sum of four byte products into a 32-bit lane) is not recognised
* `vdotu` (int8 dot, 1 words): same
* `vdotsu` (int8 dot, 1 words): same
* `vfir` (sliding window, 6 words): a window of consecutive loads times invariant taps (put8h): LoopVectorize does not vectorise it (too many live vectors), the scalar recogniser makes eight shifted streams; neither makes `vfir`
* `vclr` (accumulators, 6 words): hidden accumulation across strips: nothing reads an accumulator after the block ends (there is no vector-to-scalar move outside a block), so a block that needs one reduces inside it (`vacc` each strip), which is what both compilers do
* `vtclr` (tile and GEMM, 1 words): tile and GEMM: no loop nest is turned into tiles (`k_sgemm_nt` is vectorised on its inner dot product)
* `vtfmopa` (tile and GEMM, 2 words): same
* `vtld` (tile and GEMM, 2 words): same
* `vtst` (tile and GEMM, 2 words): same
* `vtadd` (tile and GEMM, 2 words): same
* `gemm` (tile and GEMM, 1 words): same: a call of the GEMM unit would be recognised from `llvm.matrix.multiply` or the triple loop; not done
