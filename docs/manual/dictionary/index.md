# The instruction reference tables

[← manual index](../00-index.md)

This is the reference part of the manual: **one entry for each instruction**, in the layout of a processor reference manual. Each entry gives its mnemonics, its syntax, its encoding (a chart of its layout, and the list of all its layouts), its operation in pseudocode, the flags it writes, the exceptions it can take, and where the manual explains it. The charts, the hot-register tables and the operand widths are generated from the grammars of the encoding (`spec/pdp64-enc.hbnf` and the grammar of the CPU), the one place where the encoding is written, so a layout here cannot disagree with the assembler or the simulators.

**Notation.** `Rd` is the destination register, `Rs` the first source, `Rt` the second, `Ru` the third; the assembler writes the destination last. `rm` is a general operand. `EA(rm)` is its effective address. `M[a]` is memory, `M8[a]` one byte. `sext` and `zext` sign- and zero-extend. `N Z V C` are the condition flags. Bit charts number the bits of a parcel from 15 at the left.

**Cf.** lines point to the chapter that explains the matter an instruction touches: the kernel (chapter 6), the multiprocessor model (chapter 7), the bus (chapter 2).
