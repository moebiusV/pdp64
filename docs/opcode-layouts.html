pdp64 instruction encodings, by size

Every bit layout the current ISA uses, drawn bit 15 to bit 0 per 16-bit parcel, with the number of instructions that can be encoded in it, then a table of the operands each instruction takes, followed by the one-, two- and three-parcel layouts proposed in docs/register-file-options.md §3.3–3.5 to compare against. Derived from spec/pdp64-mnemonics.hbnf and spec/pdp64.hbnf.in by assembling probe lines with build/as64 for every one of the 234 instruction words, and from the grammar where the assembler cannot yet produce a form.

Where the data differ from the brief

  1. 234 instructions in 270 mnemonic words, not 230 in 266. The grammar today has 270 words, 36 of them aliases. The four extra words are PUSHX, POPX, ISYNC and HINT (errata 37 and 39, which post-date the figures in CHANGES-SINCE-R20.md §1). pdp64-isa.md §13 already says “234 instructions in 266 words”, which does not subtract correctly (266 − 36 = 230); 234 and 270 are consistent. The 95 operations figure is the document’s and was not re-derived.
  2. TRAPL has no three-parcel form. The grammar defines it at len = 01 only (n:8 pad:8). The only layouts that exist at three parcels and nowhere else are the wide cbCC (0x29, whose 14 words also have shorter forms) and the four bit-field operations, which are the only instructions with no shorter form at all.
  3. How a form is counted. A form lists the instructions the assembler can emit in it (the shortest-length rule applies; the 22 ATOM words, the 8 MESC words, TRAPL and the four-byte SYSX forms are not yet assembled and come from the grammar). The grammar also admits longer-than-needed encodings (for instance a register escape at len = 10 with an unused third parcel, or a post-increment with a 28-bit stride); they are legal but never emitted, so they are not counted. An instruction with several forms is counted in each of them.
  4. Page 2 is not a fourth length. len = 11 is a one-parcel page of 45 defined abbreviations of 22 instructions; they are counted in the one-parcel section but add no new instruction. (The len=11 column of the payload table in CHANGES-SINCE-R20.md §4.2 is superseded by it.)

One parcel (2 bytes)

85 distinct instructions10 layouts, 104 form memberships

Ten layouts: five on page 1 (len = 00) and five on page 2 (len = 11). The five page-2 layouts share one bit partition (11 | opc6 | reg4 | low4) and differ in what reg and low mean; each is drawn because the assembler fills them differently. A page-2 form is not an extra instruction: it abbreviates a longer form of the same one, so movq counts in four of these cards but once in the subtotal.
