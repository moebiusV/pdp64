## 1. What the project is now

One CPU, the **pdp64**: thirty-two registers, prefix-coded encoding. Two earlier iterations (the original encoding r20 with sixteen registers, and a prefix-coded encoding with sixteen registers, pdp64/16) were measured against it and superseded; they are no longer CPUs and their manuals, code and tests were removed (errata 122, 123). The git tags `pre-single-cpu` (last commit with their manuals and tools) and `pre-code-removal` (last commit with their code) keep them. The comparison by which the 32-register encoding was chosen is in `docs/encoding-work/`.

**What changed since 2026-11-18** (the ledger and the manual are the reference): the machine has **no flags** (no `N Z V C`; compare-and-branch `cbCC` and `fcbCC`, the compare into a register `setCC`, a carry computed as a comparison); it is **exclusively a 32-register machine** (no `-m` option anywhere, no 16-register build of pcc); the atomics are `AADD AAND ABIS AXOR AMAX AMIN AMAXU AMINU` (the `L` form for 4 bytes), `SWAP`, `CAS`, `LL`, `SC`, with the destination last and no barrier forms, implemented in the executor, `sim64` and QEMU, and memory stays sequentially consistent; a plain load or store works at any address (a misaligned access to strongly ordered device memory is a precise fault; an atomic on uncached or device memory takes the store protection fault); one mnemonic names one operation (`trap`, `movq` for any literal, `jmp label` is `br label`, `rol` is an assembler spelling of `ror` and there is no `rori`); `MFCPUID`, `MTASID` and `MFASID` are folded into `MFSR`/`MTSR`; `FMSUB` and `FNMADD` into `FMADD` and `FNMSUB` with `FNEG`; `binary16` has conversions only; the user-memory guard (`UA`, `STUA`, `CLUA`) is gone and the `.U` moves are what remain; there are no deferred-fault loads, no `TRAPCC` (the `CT<cc>` words stay), no direct-mapping windows and no reserved `SYSPG` words; `RTI`, `RTT` and `GENTER` take a register mask; `TLBIAS` and `TLBIGP` exist; the timer is the system registers `TIME`, `TIMECMP` (zero means never) and `GTIMECMP`; the memory types are numbered cached 0, uncached 1, device weakly ordered 2, device 3 (the stricter wins, the maximum); the exit codes are 21 to 26 as in the manual; the manual's appendices are A to E and the design notes (the open, proposed and deferred matters, the history, the decisions pending) are in `docs/design/` (a second volume when buildabook has volumes).

There is one manual, [`docs/manual/`](docs/manual/00-index.md), generated from `docs/manual-src/`: the body holds decided material; the proposed, deferred and open matters and the history are in `docs/design/`. The manual describes the architecture as it is to be; the state of the implementations is in [`docs/implementation-status.md`](docs/implementation-status.md).

## 3. Rebuilding, in order

1. Host tools: a C compiler, `make`, `python3`, `ninja`, `cmake`, `git`, `pkg-config`, and for QEMU the glib and pixman development packages.
2. `tools/build-hbnf.sh` (the grammar compiler, in a rootless container), then `tools/check-all.sh`: builds `as64`, `ld64`, `sim64`, regenerates the generated files and runs the syntax, assembly, link, codec, drift, simulator, timing, relaxation and QEMU checks. It must pass before anything else is trusted.
3. The compiler: `PCC=../pcc tools/build-pcc.sh` (needs the pcc branch `single-pdp64`; it refuses a tree that still has the `PDP64_REGS32` switch), then `tools/pdp64test-all.sh` (107 programs under nine flag sets, 963 runs; `PDP64RUN=qemu` runs them under QEMU).
4. QEMU: `qemu-pdp64/build.sh`, then `tools/check-qemu.sh`.
5. The manual: `python3 tools/mkmanual.py` writes `docs/manual/` from `docs/manual-src/`; `python3 tools/mkmanual.py --check` and `tools/check-manual-src.py` check it. Never edit a generated page.
6. LLVM: `llvm-pdp64/build.sh -j N` (needs `../llvm-project` at 21.1.8; ten minutes for `llc` on 12 cores, far more on this machine), then `llvm-pdp64/check.sh`; see `llvm-pdp64/README.md`. The tables come from the grammar (`tools/gen-llvm-td.py --check`).

Assembly, linking and running: `as64 x.s; ld64 -o x.out x.o; sim64 x.out`. There is no option that selects an encoding or names the machine. The grammar is the source of truth for the encoding and is a **drop-in directory**: `spec/grammars.d/NN-name.hbnf` or `NN-name/{prologue,grammar,epilogue}.hbnf`, assembled in three passes (errata 179, 180; the contract and how a part is added are in `spec/README.md`). The two masters (`spec/pdp64/pdp64.hbnf`, `spec/pdp64-asm.hbnf`) hold directives only. The vec family is generated from `tools/vecfam.py` (change it there, not in the grammar; `check-grammar-enc.sh` fails otherwise, errata 177). Run `tools/check-all.sh` with `HBNF` and `HBNF_TEMPLATES` set to a built hbnf and its templates.

## 4. The order of work (the author, 2026-11-10; errata 125, 159) and where it stands

1. **Resolve the open items**: the opcode-affecting ones were settled enough to run the optimiser (the check instructions, the vector instructions in principle); the rest of the open items (`docs/design/open-items.md`) stay open (the ledger has the list).
2. **Re-run the encoding optimiser**: **done 2026-11-17** (errata 158, 159, 160). `tools/encopt/export.py --corpus DIR` (DIR: pcc's assembly of the 841 files, compiled with `-xchecks`), `encopt`, again with `--prev` until the selection stops changing, then `export.py --corpus DIR --write-grammar RESULT --grammar-out OUT`. The model of the optimiser is `docs/codegen-data/encoding/cat.py`.
3. **Update the grammar**: **done** (159 forms, 217 leaves, no reserve, every opcode renumbered; 1 first parcel in 65,536 is illegal). The new instructions: `ct<cc>`, `cteq`/`ctne`, `chk2`, and the vector `check` (16) of `spec/pdp64-system.hbnf`. The vector family is in the grammar (365 words, `vec`/`vec2`/`vskip`/`vend`, errata 157, 161, 170, 173, 176) and in `as64`, `sim64`, QEMU and the LLVM tables.
4. **Regenerate**: **done** for the assembler tables, the tests (`mk_vectors.py`, `mk_asm_tests.py`, `mk_ld_tests.py`), the manual (`python3 tools/mkmanual.py`), `tests/enc/generated.sha256` (`tools/check-grammar-enc.sh --update`), the QEMU copies (`qemu-pdp64/sync.sh`, then `qemu-pdp64/build.sh -j 2`, about ten minutes on two cores). The pcc header export (`tools/export-pcc-headers.sh`) must be committed on both pcc branches, `master` and `single-pdp64`; the script says which branch the checkout is on. The LLVM tables follow the grammar.
5. **Port to the new encoding**: `as64`, `ld64`, `sim64`, QEMU, pcc and the LLVM code generator are on the new encoding and green. **LLVM is to become the standard compiler for development (errata 159, R453)**: stages 1 to 2b exist (scalar code, the check instructions, vector blocks from loops, LLVM's own LoopVectorize lowered to the blocks); not done: the tile and GEMM instructions, an integrated assembler (`llc -filetype=obj`, `llvm-mc`), varargs, position-independent code (`llvm-pdp64/README.md`). This machine has two cores and 3 GB: a full clang build needs a larger one (R217).

The pcc RISC-V back end is **a separate project**, not any part of this one (the author, 2026-11-10).

After that: OpenBSD reachable over ssh (the roadmap is [`docs/roadmap.md`](docs/roadmap.md); the first operating system is OpenBSD, Unix/32V the fallback). An FFmpeg and dav1d backend (a capability bit for the vector unit and hand-written vector versions of the hot functions) is expected with the OpenBSD port.

## 6. Working rules

* **The author's decisions win**; the more recent beats the older. Each decision is recorded with his words (the register) and, if it changes a document, an erratum. When he says "looks good" or "I like it" that is a decision in favour. A decision is a decision with details pending; do not drop it because the details are not written.
* **Documents follow the ledger.** Every document is current, a dated record (with a banner) or history (with a banner); the map is `docs/README.md`. No local user names, host names, absolute paths or tool or session ids in documents. No opcode numbers or bit layouts in prose: point to the generated tables.
* The grammars are the single source of truth; every number is stated once and generated downstream; semantics once in `common/pdp64exec.c`.
* The manual describes the architecture as it is to be, not as it is.
* Web research uses the browser skill. Politeness: wait at least ten seconds between page loads on any host, more on some; stop on a captcha; after a block back off with a growing delay.
* Do not delete the author's files without asking; keep `tools/check-all.sh` green.
* Records of studies are in `docs/encoding-work/` (the map is its `README.md`): `bounds-check-isas.md`, `loongarch.md`, `check-instructions.md` (the check instructions and the table), `vector-study.md`, `vec-forms.md`, `gemm-forms.md`, `vec-cost-benefit.md`, `vec-gates.md`, `vec-isa.md`; the numbers are in `docs/encoding-work/arch-data/`, the scripts in `tools/arch-compare/` and `tools/gates/`. The decision register is `docs/decision-register.md` (the later rows win; the file says which is last), the errata `docs/errata.md` (later entries win over older text).
* A permission block from the assistant's tool is not a refusal by the author: say so plainly and ask, never write "not allowed" in a document.
